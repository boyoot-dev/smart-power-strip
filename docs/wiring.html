Power Strip Wiring

Every connection, drawn one step at a time. Build the low-voltage steps (2 to 5) first on the bench with no mains plugged in. Wire the mains (step 1) last, inside the enclosure.

220 V CAN KILL
  • Unplug from the wall before touching any wire. The screw terminals on the relay, ACS712, ZMPT101B and HLK boards are bare and live.
  • Mains wires go only through screw terminals, never through breadboard or perfboard.
  • A 16 A fuse or breaker on the input is mandatory.

The big picture

Every board has two sides. Its mains terminals sit in the red zone and its small pins sit in the green zone. The boards themselves keep the two zones apart, so no wire should ever cross the dashed line.

STEP 1 · DO THIS LAST, UNPLUGGED

Mains wiring (220 V)

Use thick wire (at least 1.5 mm², 2.5 mm² is better) and screw terminals only. Live runs through the fuse, then each outlet has its own chain: ACS712 → relay → socket. Neutral and earth go straight to the sockets.

Checklist

  • Use the relay's NO terminal. If the ESP32 fails, the outlets switch off.
  • Connect both ACS712s the same way round: supply goes into IP+.
  • Earth goes straight to both sockets. Never switch it.

Good to know

Schuko plugs fit either way round, so the "L" wire is sometimes neutral. An outlet that is switched off can still have live on one pin. Always unplug an appliance before opening it.

STEP 2 · LOW VOLTAGE

Power rails: 5 V and GND

The HLK-20M05 turns 220 V into 5 V. Run two rails on your perfboard, one for +5 V and one for GND, and connect every board to both.

STEP 3 · LOW VOLTAGE

Sensors: one resistor divider each

The sensors put out up to about 4 V, and the ESP32 takes at most 3.3 V. Two resistors scale the signal down to a safe range, and a small capacitor keeps the reading clean. Build this same little circuit three times.

ZMPT101B adjustment

The ZMPT101B board has a small blue trim knob. After the mains is connected (bring-up step 3), turn it until the output swings about ±1 V around its middle at 220 V. That leaves headroom up to 250 V.

STEP 4 · LOW VOLTAGE

Relays: one control wire each

Each relay needs 5 V, GND (from step 2) and one control wire from the ESP32. Before you power up, set the jumper on each relay board to H (high-level trigger).

STEP 5 · LOW VOLTAGE

Wall switches or touch buttons

Each outlet gets one physical control beside its socket: a rocker switch or a touch button. Choose which on the strip's web page under Settings → Outlets → Physical control (or Disabled). A rocker simply connects an ESP32 pin to GND. Use the 2-terminal switches; it doesn't matter which leg goes where, and flipping it either way toggles the outlet.

Touch button instead of a switch

A TTP223 touch module behind a blank plate cover works too. Hold a finger on the cover for 3 seconds (adjustable per outlet) and the outlet toggles; the relay click confirms it.

STEP 6 · LOW VOLTAGE

Temperature probes

Two waterproof DS18B20 probes watch the spots most likely to overheat: the screw terminals where each outlet's wire enters its ACS712 and relay. If any probe reaches 70 °C, or a probe stops answering, every outlet switches off. All probes share one data pin and one 4.7 kΩ resistor.

Before fixing them in place

  • Check the wire colours on the probe's listing. Usually red = 3V3, black = GND, yellow = data. Reversed power makes a DS18B20 very hot within seconds.
  • Power up. Within 30 seconds both probes appear on the strip's web page under Settings → Overheat protection.
  • Warm one in your hand to see which reading rises, and name it (e.g. "Out 1 terminals").

Mounting

  • Zip-tie each steel tip to the insulation of the outlet's wire, right where it enters the ACS712 or relay screw terminal.
  • Never against bare metal or a terminal screw.
  • Route the probe cable along the low-voltage side back to the ESP32.
  • If you remove or replace a probe, click Forget for it on the web page. Otherwise the strip treats it as a failed probe and switches everything off.

Testing order

1 · Bench, no mains

Do steps 2 to 6 only. Power the ESP32 from USB, and leave the HLK and all mains terminals unconnected. Check that the relays click and that the switches or touch buttons toggle them.

2 · Measure the dividers

With no load, an ACS712 OUT pin reads about 2.5 V and the ESP32 pin after its divider reads about 1.5 V. Check all three.

3 · Name the probes

Both probes should appear on the web page within 30 seconds. Warm each in your hand, name it, then zip-tie it in place.

4 · First mains test

Wire step 1 inside the closed enclosure. Test with a lamp or kettle first. Adjust the ZMPT101B knob, then calibrate from the web page.

5 · Burn-in

Run the microwave at full power for 10+ minutes. The probe readings should rise only a few degrees. Afterwards unplug, then feel the ACS712 and relay terminals and the wire joints. If anything is hot, fix it before real use.
